About FinGrowTools

Welcome to FinGrowTools, a free online utility platform dedicated to making financial planning, calculations, and digital utilities accessible to everyone. Our mission is to empower individuals, salaried professionals, and everyday investors with accurate, real-time computational tools without requiring complex spreadsheets or expensive software.

What We Offer

  • Smart Financial Calculators: Accurate tools for estimating Systematic Investment Plans (SIP), Monthly EMI calculations for personal/home loans, and modern Income Tax computations based on the latest tax slabs.
  • Browser-Based Image Utilities: Fast client-side image compression tools that optimize file size directly within your browser without sending private photos to any remote server.
  • Interactive Web Experiences: Lightweight 3D cosmic web games built using WebGL and Three.js designed for smooth performance across mobile and desktop browsers.

Privacy & Client-Side Execution

At FinGrowTools, user privacy is our core architectural focus. All financial formulas, loan amortization schedules, and graphic tools run entirely on client-side JavaScript. We do not store, harvest, or log any personal financial inputs, income figures, or uploaded media onto external databases.
