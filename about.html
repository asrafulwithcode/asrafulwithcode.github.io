About FinGrowTools

Welcome to FinGrowTools, a free online utility website that makes financial calculations and everyday digital tools easy for everyone. Our goal is to help students, salaried professionals and everyday investors get quick, accurate results without complex spreadsheets or paid software.

What We Offer

  • Financial Calculators: SIP calculator, loan EMI calculator and income tax estimator based on the current tax slabs.
  • Image & Signature Compressor: Reduce photo and signature size (for example to 50KB or 20KB) for online form uploads, directly in your browser.
  • Age Calculator: Find your exact age and check age eligibility for government job forms.

Who Runs This Site

FinGrowTools is built and maintained by an independent developer from India. We update the tools when rules, tax slabs or requirements change.

Privacy & Client-Side Execution

Our calculators and the image compressor run in your browser using JavaScript. The numbers you enter and the images you upload are not sent to or stored on our servers. For more details, read our Privacy Policy.

Disclaimer

The results from our calculators are estimates for general information only. They are not financial, tax or legal advice. Please check with a qualified professional before making financial decisions.
