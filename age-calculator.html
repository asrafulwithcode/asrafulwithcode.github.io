How Is Cut-Off Age Calculated for Government Forms?

What Is a Cut-Off Date?

For most recruitment exams your age is not checked on today's date. The official notification gives a fixed date (for example 1 January or 1 August), and you must meet the age limit on that day.

How We Count Years, Months and Days

We subtract your date of birth from the cut-off date. If the day or month of the cut-off is smaller, we borrow from the previous month, exactly like normal subtraction.

Age Relaxation

Many exams add extra years of relaxation for categories such as OBC, SC/ST and persons with disability. The extra years are added to the maximum age. Rules differ by exam, so check your notification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which date should I enter as the cut-off date?

Use the date written in the official notification under "age as on" or "cut-off date".

If the maximum age is 27, am I eligible at exactly 27 years?

In many notices the candidate must be younger than 27 years on the cut-off date, so exactly 27 years 0 months 0 days is not allowed. This calculator follows that common rule. Always check your own notification.

What if I was born on 29 February?

The age calculation works normally. For the next-birthday countdown, a non-leap year uses 1 March.

Is my date of birth saved anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
