How is Cut-Off Age Calculated for Government Forms?

Standard DD / MM / YYYY Format

In India and competitive recruitment portals, dates follow the Day-Month-Year sequence. Our calculator strictly uses this order so you never mix up months and dates while verifying your cutoff limits.

What is a Cut-Off Date?

In competitive exams like SSC, Banking, and UPSC, age is not checked as of today's date. Official notifications give a fixed benchmark date (e.g., 1st August or 1st January). You must meet the age requirements precisely on that day.
