How to Compress a Photo or Signature

  1. Choose the target size: Photo (under 50 KB) or Signature (under 20 KB).
  2. If your form asks for exact pixels, type the width and height. Otherwise leave those boxes empty.
  3. Click the box (or drag and drop) to select your JPG, PNG or WEBP image.
  4. Click Download Compressed Image and upload that file on the form.

Why Use Browser-Based Compression?

Your Files Stay on Your Device

Compression happens inside your web browser. Your photo and signature are not uploaded to our servers.

Made for Online Application Forms

Many exam and recruitment portals (for example SSC, Railway and state exams) ask for a photo under 50 KB and a signature under 20 KB. This tool lowers the image dimensions and JPEG quality step by step until the file fits under your chosen limit.

Always Check the Official Notice

Every form has its own rules for file size, pixel dimensions and format. Read the instructions on the official portal before you upload.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is processed in your browser on your own device.

How do I set exact pixel size?

Type the width and height in the two boxes before or after choosing your image. The image is centre-cropped so it does not look stretched.

The result is much smaller than 50 KB. Is that okay?

The tool picks the best quality that still fits under your limit. If your form also needs a minimum size, check its instructions.

Which format is the output?

The downloaded file is always a JPG, which is accepted by most application portals.

My PNG has a transparent background. What happens?

Transparent areas are filled with white, because JPG does not support transparency.

The tool could not reach the target size. What can I do?

Crop the image so it shows only the face or the signature, use a plainer background, or try a smaller original photo or smaller pixel size.
