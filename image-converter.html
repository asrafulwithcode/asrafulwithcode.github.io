JPG, PNG or WEBP: Which One to Choose?

JPG

Best for photographs. Files are small, and almost every website, form and phone accepts JPG. It does not support transparent backgrounds.

PNG

Best for logos, screenshots and pictures with text or a transparent background. PNG does not lose quality, but the file is often larger than a JPG.

WEBP

A modern format that usually gives smaller files than JPG or PNG at similar quality. Most modern browsers support it, but some older apps and forms do not.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion happens inside your browser on your own device.

Why is my PNG bigger than the original JPG?

PNG keeps every detail without loss, so photos usually become larger when saved as PNG. If you only need a smaller file, use JPG or WEBP, or try our Photo Compressor.

What happens to a transparent background when I convert to JPG?

JPG cannot be transparent, so transparent areas are filled with white.

Does converting a JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. Converting only changes the file format. It cannot bring back detail that is already lost, so the picture will look the same.

What happens to an animated GIF?

Only the first frame of a GIF is converted. The result is a normal still image.

The WEBP option did not work in my browser. Why?

Some browsers cannot save WEBP files. Try a recent version of Chrome, Edge or Firefox, or choose JPG or PNG instead.
