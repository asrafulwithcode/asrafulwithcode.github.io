Financial Calculators Guide & Rules

Why is Taxable Income ₹75,000 less than Gross Salary?

Under Section 16(ia) of the Income Tax Act, salaried employees and pensioners receive a flat standard deduction of ₹75,000 without needing any investment proofs. Tax is calculated strictly on this reduced taxable income.

What is the Section 87A rebate limit?

In the New Tax Regime, if your taxable income after standard deduction is up to ₹7,00,000 (meaning gross salary up to ₹7.75 lakh), you receive a 100% tax rebate under Section 87A, resulting in zero tax liability.
