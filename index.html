Financial Planning Guide & FAQ

What is a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)?

A SIP allows an investor to contribute a fixed sum regularly into mutual funds. It inculcates financial discipline and harnesses compounding and rupee cost averaging to mitigate market volatility.

How is Loan EMI Calculated?

EMI is computed using the formula: P x R x (1+R)^N / ((1+R)^N - 1), where P is loan principal, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the total months of the loan tenure.

What is the advantage of the New Tax Regime?

The revised New Tax Regime simplifies brackets, includes a standard deduction of ₹75,000 for salaried employees, and offers zero tax liability for annual incomes up to ₹7,00,000 under section 87A rebate.
