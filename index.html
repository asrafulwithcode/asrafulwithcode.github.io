How does the SIP compound return work?

Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows monthly regular investing in mutual funds, averaging market volatility through Rupee Cost Averaging and generating compounded returns over time.

What is the standard deduction in the New Tax Regime?

Salaried individuals receive an automatic ₹75,000 standard deduction under the revised New Tax Regime slabs, reducing your gross taxable income directly.
