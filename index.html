Understanding Financial Planning & Compounding

Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) allow investors to benefit from rupee cost averaging and power of compounding over extended periods. Rather than timing volatile markets, regular automated discipline builds consistent wealth portfolios.

Standard EMI Formula

EMI is calculated using: E = P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1), where P is loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is total months.

Standard Deductions

Under current financial guidelines, salaried individuals receive an upfront standard deduction against gross earnings before income slab brackets apply.
