Privacy Policy

At FinGrowTools, accessible from asrafulwithcode.github.io, the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how it is used.

Client-Side Processing & Data Safety

All utilities provided on FinGrowTools—including financial calculators (SIP, EMI, Income Tax), age calculators, and image compressors—operate entirely on the client side inside your web browser. We do not transmit, collect, or store any personal financial calculations, identity numbers, or compressed images on external servers.

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our website. It uses cookies (known as DART cookies) to serve targeted ads based upon visits to our site and other websites across the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Log Files

FinGrowTools follows a standard procedure of using log files provided by hosting platforms (GitHub Pages). These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring pages. None of this data is linked to any personally identifiable information.
